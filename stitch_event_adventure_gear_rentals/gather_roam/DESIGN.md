## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined lifestyle lens where celebration meets exploration. Built for curated event staging and wilderness expeditions alike, the aesthetic bridges the raw excitement of outdoor discovery with the elevated hospitality of bespoke gatherings. It strikes a balance between editorial poise and vibrant utility—eschewing utilitarian gear checklists in favor of structured geometry, crisp visual pacing, and premium finishes.

The target audience spans discerning event creators, weekend adventurers, and hospitality hosts who demand absolute reliability without sacrificing visual charm. The interface communicates competence, warmth, and anticipation. Generous white space, architectural alignment, and deliberate typographic rhythm frame rental kits like collector editions, turning equipment logistics into an inviting, aspirational journey.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, elevated foundation rooted in natural tones, punctuated by celebratory warmth:

- **Primary (`#1B4332`)**: Deep Forest Green serves as the anchor for key actions, brand framing, structural borders, and prominent typography. It signals dependability, organic permanence, and outdoor prestige.
- **Secondary (`#E06D53`)**: Sunset Terracotta provides festive vitality. Reserved for high-intent callouts, promotion accents, and primary conversion signals without overwhelming the composure of the screen.
- **Tertiary (`#264653`)**: Mountain Slate Blue handles informational metadata, navigation tags, and alternate category treatments.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F4F3F0`, `#FBFBFA`)**: Sand-washed, warm-tinted base backgrounds provide a tactile paper-like surface, deliberately avoiding sterile pure-grays.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure White is reserved strictly for content cards, interactive sheets, and elevated overlays to yield crisp contrast against the warm canvas.
- **Text & Foreground**: Deep Obsidian (`#191C1E`) for primary readability and Muted Charcoal (`#4A5568`) for secondary labels.
- **Status & Availability**: Emerald Green (`#2D6A4F`) for instant availability, Warm Amber (`#E7A740`) for limited allocation, and Slate Slate (`#64748B`) for fully reserved inventory.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere while maintaining immediate functional clarity for booking terms and inventory rates. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Epilogue`)**: The geometric, sculptural nature of Epilogue brings character and authority to editorial collection banners, package titles, and hero headlines. It commands attention without appearing abrasive.
- **Body & Controls (`Manrope`)**: Manrope delivers precise legibility across equipment specifications, deposit schedules, and date pickers. Its modern geometric curves harmonize seamlessly with Epilogue.
- **Numbers & Pricing**: Rental fees, deposit totals, and package inventories adopt Epilogue’s tabular and bold figures to instill confidence and commercial clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard 12-column grid on desktop screens, scaling down to an 8-column format on tablets and a 4-column flow on mobile viewports.

- **Vertical Cadence**: Built upon a consistent 8px spatial grid, layout gaps expand generously between discovery sections (`space-2xl`) to emphasize an unhurried, boutique browsing experience.
- **Alignment & Bleeds**: Product imagery may bleed to column margins in editorial features, while transactional forms, filter modules, and checkout flows remain anchored within compact, centered containers (maximum width: 1240px).
- **Responsive Handling**:
  - *Desktop (≥1024px)*: Persistent top navigation, wide multi-column facet bars, expansive package previews, side-by-side date selection.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1023px)*: Collapsible filter flyout, 2-column card layouts, inline booking selectors.
  - *Mobile (<768px)*: Single-column streams with full-width action drawers, fixed sticky booking action bars anchored to the device baseline, and edge-to-edge carousel displays with `margin-mobile` bleed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, low-intensity ambient shadows rather than stark grayscale drops, generating the impression of tactile, layered equipment cards arranged over a warm canvas:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Base canvas surfaces (`#F4F3F0`) and static utility modules sit directly on the ground plane without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest)**: Cards and panels use `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)` with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active)**: Elevated interaction layers use `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Controls / Overlays)**: Fixed booking action bars, modal drawers, and date pickers use `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(25, 28, 30, 0.12), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(25, 28, 30, 0.06)`.
- **Atmospheric Tinting**: Shadows incorporate a faint tint of Deep Forest Green (`rgba(27, 67, 50, *)`), softening transitions and tying interactive layers into the organic palette.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined radius scale (`roundedness: 2`) that balances modern elegance with approachable warmth. 

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Reserved for micro-elements, input badges, status pills, and compact table cells.
- **Standard Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px)**: Applied to product cards, primary action buttons, filter panels, and modular gear packages.
- **Elevated Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px)**: Used for high-impact visual sheets, hero feature blocks, mode switches, and full-screen checkout modal wrappers.
- **Pill Utility**: Fully circular borders (`9999px`) are exclusively used for status badges, counter dots, and category toggle triggers to maintain distinct visual semantics.

## Components

### Mode Switch Pill Toggle
- **Structure**: Encapsulated twin-segment pill container floating on a subtle frosted background.
- **States**: The active mode ("🎉 Events & Parties" or "🏕️ Trips & Camping") is highlighted with a crisp white pill surface, subtle Level 1 shadow, and bold Epilogue typography. The inactive state remains neutral charcoal text with an effortless hover transition.

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Forest Green background, crisp white label, generous internal padding (`0.75rem 1.5rem`), `rounded-lg` radius. Hover shifts to a deep pine glow with subtle Level 2 shadow.
- **Accent / Book Now**: Warm Sunset Terracotta background with bold contrast for rental reservation actions.
- **Secondary / Outline**: 1.5px border colored with Forest Green at 20% opacity, transparent background, interactive darkening on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges**: Pill-shaped with subtle tinted backgrounds (e.g., Emerald Green tint for "Available", Amber tint for "Limited Stock", Slate tint for "Rented Out"). Paired with uppercase `label-sm` typography and a leading 6px colored indicator dot.
- **Filter Chips**: Soft white background, subtle border, interactive toggle state filled with Deep Forest Green and inverted text when selected.

### Cards & Package Summaries
- **Equipment & Package Cards**: White background, `rounded-lg` silhouette, continuous subtle hairline border. High-aspect product photography with inset availability chips.
- **Pricing Footers**: Prominent daily rate formatted in `price-tag` styling paired with inline duration terms ("per weekend" or "per day") and a clear call-to-action button.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- **Text & Date Fields**: Minimalist warm-white inputs featuring inset labels, 1.5px structural borders, and a focused ring in Deep Forest Green. 
- **Calendar & Range Selectors**: Range segments link together with an uninterrupted terracotta-tinted wash between selected reservation dates, capped by solid endpoints.

### Sticky Booking Action Bar
- **Application**: Locked to the bottom viewport on mobile and tablet displays.
- **Layout**: Displays real-time rental pricing and date windows on the left; positions an immediate "Reserve Gear" call-to-action on the right. Elevated using Level 3 ambient shadow with frosted backdrop blur.

### Wizard Checkout Steps
- **Progression Flow**: A clean numbered track featuring minimal connector lines that switch from Sand Grey to Deep Forest Green upon step validation.
- **Content Panels**: Contained in `rounded-xl` summary cards highlighting gear safety deposits, delivery windows, and item verification checklists.
