Curator's Field Notes

The Timberline Grand represents the pinnacle of experiential outdoor lodging. Designed for festival hostings, wilderness bridal suites, and restorative family campouts, its conical design efficiently diffuses thermal heat while retaining wind stability through an octagonal foundation. Unlike synthetic polyester tents that trap condensation and humidity, our pure army duck canvas lets the shelter breathe naturally during scorching midday sun while retaining soothing ambient warmth after dark.

air Microclimate Flow

Four mesh-screened ground vents and three hooded apex vents facilitate constant convective airflow without bug ingress.

thunderstorm Treated Canvas

Proprietary natural wax infusion repels severe mountain cloudbursts while resisting mildew, ember sparks, and mold spores.

speed Single-Pole Pivot

The center-mast telescopic aluminum pole allows two individuals to pitch the entire structure in under 20 operational minutes.
